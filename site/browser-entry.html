Proposed entry sequence

The following is the user's proposed sequence, not a successful run recorded by this workspace. Do not change DNS simply to close a phase gate. Only proceed if you intentionally choose to test a third-party DNS service and understand the network trust change.

  1. Open System Settings on your Switch 2.
  2. Go to Internet, then Internet Settings.
  3. Select your current Wi-Fi network, then Change Settings.
  4. Change DNS Settings from Automatic to Manual.
  5. Enter 045.055.142.122 as Primary DNS (the service address is 45.55.142.122).
  6. Save and select Connect to this Network.
  7. If a registration-required prompt appears, select OK or Next to open the portal. If it does not appear, record the failure; do not update firmware to force it.
  8. If the portal supports custom URLs, open https://shubin123.github.io/switch2jailbreak/browser-entry.html.

Record previous DNS settings before making changes and restore them when finished. Do not enter account credentials into the third-party portal or disable TLS/security controls. Portal availability and behavior on firmware 23.0.1 remain unverified here.
