01 / Outstanding evidence

Green CI is not console attestation.

01

No target browser access

Desktop Chromium, WebKit, and Firefox controls pass. No public WebKit entry point or real Switch 2 browser telemetry has been established. Desktop results do not establish target compatibility.

02

No hardware execution attestation

Host-side parser, descriptor, and syscall mock checks verify interface contracts. They do not prove console exploit execution or hardware compatibility.

03

Device recovery remains unproven

Recovery from aborts, network disconnections, or incomplete payload mappings has not been exercised on a physical console.

04

Native review is incomplete

Selected native paths have a documented walkthrough and 36 host-only checks. Remaining native-chain paths and unresolved assumptions still require review at the pinned revision.
